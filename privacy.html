Privacy Policy
Plantelo

Last updated: November 15, 2026 · Português

Summary: Plantelo needs no account, name, email or location. The photo you choose to identify a plant is sent for identification and is not stored by us. Your garden, reminders and journal stay only on your phone. The app shows Google AdMob ads, which use the device's advertising ID.

1. Who we are

Plantelo is a free app by JPFG Apps that identifies plants from photos, shows care tips and reminds you to water and fertilize. It is distributed in Brazil and follows Brazil's data protection law (LGPD).

2. What we do NOT collect

  • No name, email, phone number, ID or sign-up.
  • No location, contacts or phone files.
  • No access to your photo library: we only receive the photo you take or pick to identify.
  • We do not sell data.

3. Plant identification (photo)

The app shrinks the photo on your phone and sends it to our proxy server (hosted on Cloudflare), which forwards it to the Pl@ntNet identification service. We do not store the photo; Pl@ntNet may retain images under the Pl@ntNet terms. A random install number (which does not identify you) counts your daily free identifications and is deleted within 2 days. Your IP address is used only during the request, for security, and is not stored by us.

4. Stored only on your phone

Your plants, nicknames and photos, reminders, watering history, journal notes, preferences and past identification results. They are deleted when you remove a plant, uninstall the app or clear its data.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

A small banner and an optional rewarded video that unlocks more identifications. Google AdMob may use the advertising ID, technical device information and ad interactions. Where required, Google's consent form is shown; you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices and reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

6. Store links (affiliate)

Some plant pages suggest care products with links to stores such as Mercado Livre. We may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We count anonymously how often each product is tapped and which identified species are missing from our care guide, with no identifier of yours.

7. Permissions

  • Internet: send the photo for identification, load ads and update the product list.
  • Notifications: care reminders scheduled on the phone itself; no push server.
  • Start at boot: reschedule reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob.

The app does not request camera or photo-library permission: photos come from Android's own camera app or system photo picker.

8. Children

The app is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13.

9. Your rights

You may request information, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent by emailing the address below.

10. Disclaimer

Identification can be wrong. Care and toxicity information is for guidance only and does not replace a veterinarian, doctor or professional. Never eat a plant based on the app's identification.
